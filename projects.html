Digital Competency Mock App

2026
  • Work
  • Research

A mock health-record web app used to assess the digital competency of frontline health workers (ASHAs, ANMs, etc.) in the Indian public health system. It mimics the look and basic functionality of a real reporting app (e.g. UWIN). During a competency survey, a health worker fills in a record for a mock patient case, and the app captures interaction metrics (time per field, edits, errors) for later analysis.
