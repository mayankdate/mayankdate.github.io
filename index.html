About

Public health researcher trained at Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, specializing in epidemiologic methods, measurement of population health indicators, and digital health systems. I lead analytics for multi-country projects at the Center for Global Digital Health Innovations with partners including UNICEF, the World Bank, and the ITU. I'm preparing for doctoral training to advance measurement science and build scalable tools that strengthen how global health programs are run.
